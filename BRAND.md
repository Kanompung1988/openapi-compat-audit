# Area Zero brand asset policy

The images `assets/area-zero-logo-full.svg` and `assets/area-zero-logo-icon.svg` are the **unaltered official marks** from the Areazero owner, with verified SHA-256 hashes. They are provided only to identify this project; they are **not** relicensed by this repo's MIT License.

- Do not treat these logos or the Area Zero name as permission to impersonate, resell, or sublicense the brand.
- If forking, retain code under MIT while substituting your own branding unless authorized.
- Brand colors: cyan `#00D9FF`, mint `#00FFA3`, navy `#0A1929`. Keep the original SVG's paths, gradients, proportions and clear space intact.
- The original, unique `assets/hero.svg` illustration is documentation artwork, not a replacement for the official mark.
